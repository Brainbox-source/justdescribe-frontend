Why JustDescribe was built

I faced a problem and could not accept it as normal.

In my first year at Miva, I needed to defer my semester and change my course, so I had to raise a ticket. I did not know which options to pick in the form, so I asked ChatGPT.

I didn’t want to continue that way. I collaborated with Peter to build a small demo of JustDescribe to show our coursemates. They found it really useful.

Sometimes you just have to show people what’s possible.

Precious Levi, Founder
How it works

Three steps. No prompts.

Chrome extension, for laptops Coming soon
  1. 01

    Sign in

    Use your Miva student email.

  2. 02

    Describe it

    Write your issue or enquiry in your own words. That is all you type.

  3. 03

    Click Fill

    One click fills the ticket form for you. You review it and submit it yourself.

Web app, for phones
  1. 01

    Sign in

    Use your Miva student email.

  2. 02

    Describe it

    Write your issue or enquiry in your own words. That is all you type.

  3. 03

    Copy it in

    Copy your subject, classification, case, nature of case and description into the ticket form. Then review it and submit it yourself.
