Last updated: 15/11/2026

These terms explain the rules for using JustDescribe. By signing in or using JustDescribe, you agree to them. If you don't agree, please don't use it.

What JustDescribe is

JustDescribe is a free AI assistant for Miva Open University students. You describe your issue or inquiry, and JustDescribe suggests a subject, a classification, a nature of case, a case, and a cleaned-up description for the Miva support ticket form. It is available as a web app and a Chrome extension.

We are not Miva

JustDescribe is built by students. We do not raise tickets for you and we have no say in how Miva handles them. You submit your ticket yourself on the Miva support portal after JustDescribe gives you all you need.

Who can use it

JustDescribe is for Miva students. You sign in with your Miva Google account. You must be a current student with a valid Miva student email. You are responsible for your own account.

Check the suggestions before you use them

The suggestions come from an AI. AI can be wrong. Before you submit your ticket, read every field and make sure it matches your issue. You are responsible for what you submit to Miva. We are not responsible for a ticket that is sent to the wrong place or handled late because a suggestion was wrong.

What you can and can't do

Please do:

  • Describe real issues and inquiries that you want to raise with Miva.

Please don't:

  • Use JustDescribe for anything unlawful, abusive, or harmful.
  • Try to break it, overload it, or get around its limits.
  • Try to get into another person's account or data.
  • Use bots or scripts to send requests in bulk.
  • Pretend to be someone else, or sign in with an account that isn't yours.

What you send us

You own the text you write. When you send a description, we pass it to a third-party AI provider so it can produce the suggestions. We do not save your ticket text or your results. Our Privacy Policy explains what we do keep and how we use it. Please read it with these terms.

Do not put passwords, bank card details, or other secrets in your description. You don't need them to raise a ticket.

We may change limits, change features, or stop the service when Miva's own AI, MIND, is in production and built directly into the support portal or your LMS to handle your tickets.

The Chrome extension also depends on how the Miva support portal looks and works. If Miva changes its form, the one-click fill may stop working until we update it.
