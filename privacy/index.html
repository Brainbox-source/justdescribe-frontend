Privacy Policy

Last updated: 04/11/2026

JustDescribe is built by Levi Precious and Peter God’spower, students at Miva Open University. You can reach us at JustDescribe HQ.

What we do not keep

We do not save your ticket text or your results.

What happens to what you type

Your text goes to our server. Our server sends it to Groq, the AI service that writes your subject, classification, case, nature of case and refined description. We then send the answer back to you. Groq does not keep your text.

The extension

It only works on Miva’s support ticket page, and only when you ask it to fill the form. It does not read your other tabs or your browsing. It stores your sign-in and nothing else.

Who else is involved

We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page.
